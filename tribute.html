Olivia Rodrigo

About Olivia

Olivia Rodrigo (born February 20, 2003) is an American singer-songwriter and actress. She rose to fame on Disney Channel and gained worldwide recognition with her debut single "drivers license" in 2021.

Key Contributions

  • Shaped Gen Z pop with emotional storytelling.
  • Inspired new singer-songwriters worldwide.
  • Won 3 Grammy Awards early in her career.
  • Brought pop-punk influences back to charts.
